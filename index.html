Project Overview

NS Auto Center is a real-world business automation project designed to digitize and streamline the daily operations of a two-wheeler garage and spare-parts shop. The system manages customer bookings, spare-parts catalog, billing and challan generation, service reminders, customer notifications, and payment workflow planning — converting a fully manual operation into a scalable digital platform.

AI Integration & Automation Role
  • Applied prompt engineering to convert business ideas and owner requirements directly into structured product specifications and feature roadmaps.
  • Orchestrated multiple AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Codex, Replit) to accelerate planning, code generation, debugging, and documentation.
  • Used AI-assisted development workflows to design business logic for appointment scheduling, invoice generation, service reminders, and customer journeys — significantly reducing development cycle time.
  • Practiced AI tool orchestration and prompt iteration to improve output quality across each module.
  • Translated ambiguous, non-technical client requirements into precise digital workflows using AI as a bridge layer.
