Client Project 2025 — Ongoing
NS Auto Center
Flagship Case Study · AI Shop Automation System for a Two-Wheeler Garage
The Problem

NS Auto Center ran entirely on manual processes — paper appointments, handwritten billing, no digital customer records, and no automated follow-ups after service. The owner needed a single system to manage bookings, spare-parts sales, invoicing, and customer communication without hiring a full tech team.

The Solution

I built an end-to-end shop automation platform that digitizes daily garage operations into connected workflows — from the moment a customer books a service to billing, parts sales, and post-service reminders.

  • Service booking system for full service, wash, spare-parts fitting, and engine repair.
  • Spare-parts catalog with cart, checkout, and e-commerce-style customer experience.
  • Billing and challan generation for both product purchases and service invoices.
  • Customer notifications and service reminder logic for retention and repeat visits.
  • Admin panel for shop workflows, plus planned eSewa/Khalti payment integration.
  • Loyalty and reward system architecture for long-term engagement.
Automation Workflows
  • Book → Service → Bill: Customer books online → shop assigns service → auto-generated invoice/challan.
  • Parts → Cart → Pay: Browse catalog → checkout → payment workflow (eSewa/Khalti planned).
  • Service → Remind → Return: Post-service reminders drive repeat bookings and retention.
  • Owner → Spec → Ship: AI-assisted requirements translation into buildable modules, fast iteration cycles.
